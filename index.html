God Orders Our Steps

When I think about the way God orders our steps, I think about how we don't always understand why certain people cross our paths or why certain things happen when they do. Sometimes we only understand it later, when we look back and realise that God was doing something all along.

And honestly, that's how I feel about you.

I don't even know where to begin with this, but I just feel so grateful that God blessed me with you.

I've yearned for a man like you for so many years, and that's why I always say that you're an answered prayer. You probably already know this because you and I have talked about it so many times, but I still want you to know that you're not just a man who happens to be my boyfriend. You're so much more than that to me.

You've become one of those people I can talk to about anything and everything without being afraid of being judged, or feeling like the other person is above me and somehow making me feel small. And I really value that about us.

I also love how much we have in common. I love that you're my best friend. I really do.

Sometimes I think back to how all of this started, and I honestly had no idea that you would become this important to me. I think you know that already. You actually know so much about how I feel because you and I talk about our relationship so much. There isn't really much here that is new to you because these are things I constantly remind you of.

But I still wanted to put them here.

Because I know relationships come with their difficult moments, and I know we'll have times that challenge us and require us to understand each other better.

I just hope that through those moments, we continue to learn, grow, and let God guide us.

I just pray that God continues to be at the centre of what we have, and that He gives us the wisdom and grace to navigate every season together.

I'm really grateful that our paths crossed, and that I get to call you mine. I'm grateful that I get to know you, love you, learn from you, and experience this part of life with you.

I see the love that you pour into me. I notice the little things you do, the things you teach me, and everything you pour into my cup. None of it goes unnoticed, and I hope you always know how much I appreciate it.

I could probably write a whole book about you because there are so many things I could say and so many little things I've noticed about you. But a lot of those things are already part of the conversations we have and the way we speak into each other's lives.

So maybe this doesn't have to say everything.

Maybe it just needs to remind you of this:

I love you. I appreciate you. And I'm genuinely grateful that God allowed our paths to cross.

I pray that He continues to order our steps.

From my heart

A Prayer Over Your Life

I think after everything I've said, there is one more thing I want to give you, a prayer.

Not just for your birthday, but for the life you're stepping into and everything that is still ahead of you.

So, from my mouth to God's ears, this is what I pray over your life.

I pray that the Lord blesses you and keeps you, that He makes His face shine upon you, is gracious towards you, and gives you peace. I pray that His presence goes with you wherever you go.

I pray that, like David in Psalm 27, you will always be able to say that the Lord is your light and your salvation. When life gets difficult or you don't know what the next step looks like, may you continue to seek Him and trust Him to lead you.

I pray that God gives you wisdom like He gave Daniel, wisdom to make the right decisions, understand what you're learning, and navigate every area of your life. When things don't go according to plan, may He give you the understanding to see beyond the moment and recognise what He may be teaching you through it.

And when you face difficult seasons, may Psalm 91 always remind you where your safety is. May you find refuge in God and know that He is watching over you. May He command His angels concerning you, to guard you in all your ways.

I pray that you never lose your desire to know God for yourself. In every season, I pray that you seek first His kingdom and His righteousness, trusting Him with everything else.

As you enter another year of your life, I pray that you grow closer to Him, grow in wisdom and courage, and become everything He has created you to be.

I pray that He protects your mind, your heart, your plans, and your purpose, and gives you the strength and humility to keep going and return to Him whenever you need direction.

And above everything, I pray that you never get so caught up in building your life that you forget the One who gave you the life you're building.

Happy birthday, Handsome.
